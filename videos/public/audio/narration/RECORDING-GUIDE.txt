RECORDING GUIDE — Rarity Reel narration (Seiko 6309)
====================================================

Record the 7 takes below, one per line, in a quiet room with your phone about
15–20 cm from your mouth. Speak in a long, flowing, conversational tone — like
you're telling a friend about the watch. DON'T read it sentence-by-sentence;
run each take as one continuous thought. The edit will cut scenes on your voice.

SAVE AS (exact names, replacing the current MP3s):
  public/audio/narration/rarity-01-hook.mp3
  public/audio/narration/rarity-02-reframe.mp3
  public/audio/narration/rarity-03-caliber.mp3
  public/audio/narration/rarity-04-legacy.mp3
  public/audio/narration/rarity-05-detail.mp3
  public/audio/narration/rarity-06-auth.mp3
  public/audio/narration/rarity-07-cta.mp3

MP3, M4A or WAV all work. Trim silence at the start/end of each take if you can.

THE LINES
---------
01 — Hook
  "Every so often, a watch shows up that makes collectors stop and look
   twice — this one is hiding a part Seiko stopped making in
   nineteen eighty-eight."

02 — Reframe
  "Everyone assumes an automatic watch is automatically rare, but that is
   not how rarity works — what matters is what is inside."

03 — Signal 01, The Caliber
  "Here is the signal collectors care about most — the movement. This runs
   a genuine six three oh nine: seventeen jewels, twenty-one thousand six
   hundred beats per hour. Seiko stopped making it in eighty-eight and
   never made a replacement."

04 — Signal 02, The Legacy
  "This movement powered the legendary Turtle divers collectors still chase
   today. When Seiko revived the Turtle in twenty-fifteen, they had to fit
   a new movement — the six three oh nine was long gone."

05 — Signal 03, The Detail
  "Here is the detail collectors actually check. Turn the watch over — the
   caseback engraving tells you exactly what is inside, and that is how you
   verify a movement."

06 — Authentication
  "Every piece on the Collectors Exchange goes through this before it is
   listed — movement inspection, caseback verification, serial cross-check
   — only then does it go live, authenticated."

07 — Call to action
  "This one is live right now — a Seiko six three oh nine automatic at
   forty-eight hundred rupees. If it speaks to you, find it on our website,
   the Collectors Exchange."

AFTER SAVING YOUR TAKES
-----------------------
Run from the videos/ folder:

  node tools/measure-narration.mjs
  npx remotion render RarityReel out/tce-rarity-reel-9x16.mp4 --codec=h264

The first command re-measures your clips and rewrites the timing data the
video reads. The render then re-times every scene to YOUR voice automatically.
